# Positioning

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-24
**Status:** MVP positioning locked 2026-06-19 (see [[Roadmap-2026-06-19-Review-Board]]).

## MVP Target — Product Managers Indonesia (single segment)

The dual-segment plan (Vibe Coders + PMs) is **deferred post-MVP.** The MVP is focused on **one**
buyer to keep messaging and the product sharp:

> **For Indonesian Product Managers who get vague, incomplete BRDs from stakeholders —
> StoryForge turns a messy BRD into a gap list, clarification questions, a readiness score,
> and a build-ready PRD in minutes, so sprints don't stall on ambiguity.**

## Key Messages
- **Stop sprint delays caused by ambiguous BRDs** — find the gaps before engineering does.
- **Readiness Score 0-100** — an objective "is this ready to build?" signal to show stakeholders.
- **Bahasa-first** — clarification questions are copy-paste ready for Indonesian stakeholders.
- **Privacy** — zero-data-retention path; your BRDs aren't used to train models.

## Deferred (post-MVP)
- Segment A: Vibe Coders / Non-Technical Founders (idea → PRD for vibe coding).
